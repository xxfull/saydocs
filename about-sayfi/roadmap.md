# Roadmap

### TradingView integration

YesFi plans to integrate TradingView to deliver richer charting and technical-analysis tools. The integration is currently under application and remains subject to approval.

Once available, it will help traders evaluate markets, test ideas, and act without leaving YesFi.

#### Real-time market analysis

Explore price action through professional candlestick charts and technical indicators. Track market movements across supported assets and identify trends with greater clarity.

View the [BTCUSD](https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/BTCUSD/).

#### Strategy testing

Use historical market data to test trading ideas before committing capital. Backtesting can help you refine entries, exits, and risk parameters.

#### A unified trading workflow

Access TradingView tools directly from the YesFi trading interface. Keep analysis and execution in one workflow, with fewer context switches between platforms.

{% hint style="info" %}
This integration is planned. Features, availability, and release timing may change before launch.
{% endhint %}
