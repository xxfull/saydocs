### 4. Product-Specific Risks

#### 4.1 Flash Options

Flash Options are short-duration conditional payout products. If the applicable condition is not satisfied in accordance with the product rules, you may lose the entire premium or other amount paid.

Because Flash Options have short durations and condition-based outcomes, small price movements, brief market events, timing differences, or minor variations in market data may determine the result.

A quoted payout does not represent the probability of success, expected return, fair value, or a guarantee that the applicable condition will be satisfied.

Pricing and payouts may depend on volatility, duration, reference prices, correlations, liquidity, market conditions, user positioning, risk limits, models, and other factors. Terms may differ between users or change over time.

Unless the applicable product rules expressly permit otherwise, you may not be able to cancel, transfer, close, or exit a Flash Option after confirmation.

Settlement will be determined according to the applicable trigger, observation period, reference source, calculation method, and settlement rules. Any displayed result before final settlement may be provisional.

#### 4.2 Insurance Products

“Insurance Products” is a product label used within the Services for convenience only. The use of this term does not determine or imply the legal or regulatory classification of any product.

Insurance Products are conditional protection products subject to eligibility requirements, trigger conditions, exclusions, payout limits, settlement conditions, reserve availability, and other product-specific rules.

The purchase or existence of an Insurance Product does not guarantee that a payout will become due, that settlement will occur immediately, or that all losses will be recovered.

A trading loss, liquidation, price movement, account status, or displayed Insurance Product status does not by itself establish payout eligibility.

A displayed product or settlement status does not necessarily mean that settlement is final or that funds have been received and finally confirmed on-chain.

Availability, premiums, protection limits, issuance capacity, and settlement timing may be affected by reserve capacity, market stress, network conditions, compliance checks, security reviews, technical failures, and applicable risk controls.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, Insurance Products are not protected by any government-backed insurance scheme, policyholder protection arrangement, insurance compensation fund, or deposit guarantee.

Insurance Products do not eliminate the risks associated with the underlying trading product. You should review the applicable Insurance Product Terms before purchasing or relying on an Insurance Product.

#### 4.3 Synthetic Exposures

Synthetic exposures provide economic exposure to a referenced asset or market but do not give you ownership of the underlying asset.

You do not receive shareholder rights, dividends, voting rights, redemption rights, delivery rights, governance rights, information rights, or other ownership rights in the referenced asset.

Reference instruments may be affected by market closures, limited trading hours, trading suspensions, corporate actions, index rebalancing, stock splits, dividends, mergers, insolvency events, regulatory restrictions, or changes in pricing methodology.

For Pre-IPO or other privately held reference assets, reliable public pricing may be limited or unavailable. Reference values may be based on estimates, secondary-market information, models, third-party sources, or other methodologies.

A synthetic price may differ materially from an eventual financing price, acquisition price, listing price, public-market price, or price available through another venue.

Synthetic prices may diverge from related markets, particularly when the referenced market is closed, suspended, illiquid, or experiencing disruption.
