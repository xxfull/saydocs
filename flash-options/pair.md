# Pair

### **Rules** <a href="#ht64s" id="ht64s"></a>

* Compare the relative strength of two assets. Pick any asset pair and predict which one will outperform the other within the selected time period.
* Duration options: 5 minutes or 15 minutes
* Minimum amount: 10u. Maximum amount depends on the tier level, ranging from 10u to 5000u.
* Available asset pairs:
* 24/7 perpetual market: BTC vs ETH, BTC vs Gold, Gold vs Crude Oil, BNB vs HYPE
* US market session: Nvidia vs BTC, Nvidia vs Gold
* Asia session: BTC vs JPY, Gold vs JPY

### **Example gameplay (5 minutes)** <a href="#id-516f8d66" id="id-516f8d66"></a>

At 14:00:00, the user enters the "Nvidia vs Gold" matchup and bets that Nvidia will outperform Gold over 5 minutes.

At 14:05, the result is settled: Nvidia rises 0.22% in 5 minutes, while Gold rises 0.03%. Nvidia outperforms by a net 0.19%, which exceeds the extreme tier threshold of 0.18%. A 10u bet on the extreme tier returns 140u.
