# Membership Levels and Benefits

Complete tasks and invite friends to earn Y Points and level up automatically. Your level determines your weekly options vouchers and blind boxes.

This page explains how to:

1. Understand membership levels and progress through them.
2. Earn Y Points through tasks and referrals.
3. Check and use your rewards.

### Membership levels

Membership levels range from **V1 to V40** across five tiers:

| Tier       | Level range |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| Bronze     | V1–V5       |
| Platinum   | V6–V15      |
| Black Gold | V16–V25     |
| Diamond    | V26–V35     |
| Oracle     | V36–V40     |

Higher levels provide more valuable weekly options vouchers and blind boxes.

#### How to level up

Complete tasks in the **Task Center** to earn **Y Points**. Your level upgrades automatically when you reach its Y Point threshold.

More advanced tasks and more active trading help you earn Y Points faster.

{% hint style="info" %}
Complete all beginner and advanced tasks to earn 10,960Y. With daily challenges, you can reach V6's 15,000Y threshold in about 41 days.
{% endhint %}

Refer to the **Membership Levels** page for each level's current Y Point threshold.

#### Promotion packages

The first time you reach **V6, V16, V26, or V36**, you receive a one-time promotion package in addition to that week's regular benefits.

Each package includes options vouchers and blind boxes. Each promotion package is issued once per user and separately from weekly benefits.

#### Benefit issuance rules

* **Automatic issuance:** No manual claim is needed. Benefits are issued automatically to the Reward Center.
* **Current-level issuance:** If you level up during a week, your new level applies from the **next benefit cycle**. The current week is not adjusted retroactively.
* **Activity requirement:** Weekly benefits pause after 14 consecutive days without a valid trade. Complete one valid trade to restore eligibility from the next benefit cycle. Benefits missed during the pause are not reissued.
* **Abnormal accounts:** The system reviews account activity. Accounts that trigger anomaly rules may not receive benefits.

#### FAQ: levels and benefits

**Q: How do I level up?**\
A: Complete beginner tasks, advanced tasks, daily challenges, or referrals to earn Y Points. Your level upgrades automatically when you meet the threshold.

**Q: When are level benefits issued?**\
A: Benefits are issued once per week to the Reward Center. No manual claim is required.

**Q: Why did I not receive benefits this week?**\
A: Check whether you completed a valid trade in the last 14 days. Benefits pause during inactivity and resume in the next benefit cycle after you trade again.

### Earning Y Points

#### How to earn

Connect a wallet, complete your profile, trade, and invite friends to earn **Y Points** for membership progression.

{% hint style="info" %}
A deposit alone does not earn Y Points or count toward tasks. You must complete an actual trade to unlock related rewards.
{% endhint %}

#### Task Center

**Beginner tasks**

Complete every task below to earn **2,000Y** and automatically reach V1:

| Task                                                    | Reward     |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Connect a wallet successfully for the first time        | 100Y       |
| Verify your email and complete required profile details | 200Y       |
| Complete your first valid trade (premium/margin ≥10U)   | 700Y       |
| Complete your second valid trade                        | 350Y       |
| Complete your third valid trade                         | 650Y       |
| **Complete all tasks**                                  | **2,000Y** |

Your first valid trade must be at least 10U. Your second and third trades have no additional amount requirement. Beginner task rewards can only be earned once.

**Advanced tasks**

Advanced tasks unlock after you complete all beginner tasks:

| Task                                                           | Reward     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Valid trade amount ≥50U                                        | 50Y        |
| Valid trade amount ≥300U                                       | 360Y       |
| Valid trade amount ≥500U                                       | 700Y       |
| Valid trade amount ≥1,000U                                     | 1,600Y     |
| Valid trade amount ≥3,000U                                     | 5,400Y     |
| Trade for 3 consecutive days (≥1 valid trade daily)            | 300Y       |
| Trade for 7 consecutive days (after completing the 3-day task) | 550Y       |
| **Complete all tasks**                                         | **8,960Y** |

Trade amount tiers use the highest-tier settlement rule. A 3,000U order earns all uncompleted rewards for tiers it covers. Each tier reward can only be earned once.

**Daily challenges**

Daily challenges unlock the day after you complete beginner tasks. They reset daily at 00:00 UTC.

| Task                                                    | Reward   |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Daily start: complete 1 valid trade                     | 20Y      |
| Daily boost: complete 1 valid trade with an amount ≥50U | 30Y      |
| Daily streak: complete ≥3 valid trades                  | 50Y      |
| **Daily maximum**                                       | **100Y** |

Daily challenges can be completed again each day. Advanced tasks are one-time achievements. You can earn rewards from both types at the same time.

**What is a valid trade?**

A valid trade is an options or contract order completed with **your own funds**. It must meet all of these conditions:

* The order premium or margin is at least 10 USDC.
* The order has settled and was not cancelled or refunded.
* The order did not use a voucher or platform subsidy.

Orders using vouchers can trade and settle normally. They do not count toward tasks, level progress, or leaderboards.

#### Invite friends

**Referral milestones**

These rewards are based on your total successful referrals:

| Milestone                      | Reward                                           |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Successfully invite 1 friend   | 50Y                                              |
| Successfully invite 5 friends  | 300Y + 5U options voucher                        |
| Successfully invite 10 friends | 800Y + 10U options voucher + blind box ×1        |
| Successfully invite 20 friends | 2,000Y + 20U options voucher + blind box ×2      |
| **Complete all milestones**    | **3,150Y + 35U options vouchers + blind box ×3** |

**Per-friend quality rewards**

Each referred friend can generate these cumulative rewards:

| Task                                                                    | Reward                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Friend completes their first deposit, of any amount                     | 150Y                                           |
| Friend completes their first real trade                                 | 150Y + 5U options voucher + blind box ×1       |
| Friend trades on 10 days and reaches 1,000U total volume within 30 days | 250Y + 15U options voucher + blind box ×2      |
| **Maximum per friend**                                                  | **550Y + 20U options vouchers + blind box ×3** |

**Definitions**

* **Successful referral:** A friend completes one settled, non-cancelled trade. That trade may use a voucher.
* **First real trade:** A friend's first trade completed with **their own funds**. Voucher orders do not qualify.
* **Valid trading day:** A friend completes at least one real trade in a calendar day. Any number of trades on that day counts as one day.
* **Long-term active friend:** Within 30 days of their first real trade, a friend completes at least 10 valid trading days and reaches 1,000U in total trading volume.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Referrers and referred friends must be different account holders. If the system identifies them as the same user, neither the referral nor its rewards count.
{% endhint %}

### Using rewards

#### Options voucher rules

* **Eligible products:** Vouchers only offset premiums for standard options, including Trend, Ladder, Pair, and Combo products. They cannot be used for Instant options, volatility products, range products, contracts, or insurance products.
* **How to use:** Each order can use one voucher. Vouchers cannot be combined with other vouchers or benefits. They apply in full, with no change or refund.
* **Minimum requirement:** The order premium must meet the voucher's minimum requirement. For example, a 5U voucher requires a 10U minimum premium. You pay any remaining balance.
* **Voucher orders:** These orders can trade and settle normally. They **do not count** toward beginner tasks, successful referrals or referral rewards, level progress, trading-day or volume tasks, blind-box eligibility, or leaderboards. Use your own funds for qualifying orders.
* **Expiry:** Use vouchers before they expire. Expired vouchers cannot be extended, exchanged for cash, or reissued.
* **Invalid orders:** If an order is cancelled, refunded, reversed, or ruled invalid, voucher returns depend on the order status and the voucher details page. Used vouchers are generally not refunded, reissued, or exchanged for change.

#### Blind box rules

* Blind boxes are available through task rewards, level benefits, referral rewards, and selected benefits. They cannot be purchased directly.
* Blind boxes contain **Y Points** and **options vouchers**. They do not include cash, insurance vouchers, or other benefit types.
* Higher levels provide more blind boxes and higher expected reward values.

#### FAQ: tasks and rewards

**Q: Does a deposit count as a task?**\
A: A deposit alone does not count toward beginner, advanced, or daily tasks. Complete an actual trade to receive the related reward. A referred friend's deposit may earn a separate referral reward.

**Q: Can I complete tasks with a voucher order?**\
A: No. Voucher orders can trade normally, but do not count toward tasks, level progress, referral rewards, or leaderboards. Use your own funds to complete tasks.

**Q: When are task rewards issued?**\
A: After an order settles, the system automatically evaluates and issues the relevant reward. No manual claim is required.
