# Rewards rules

The YesFi membership program is built around **Membership Points (Y Points)** as its core growth metric. User activities on the platform — including wallet connection, trading, daily check-ins, and referrals — are converted into Y Points according to defined rules. Accumulated Y Points determine a user's membership level, and membership level in turn determines the benefits a user is entitled to.

***

### 1. How the Program Works

The membership program operates as a closed loop:

{% hint style="info" icon="rotate" %}
**Complete a task → Earn Y Points → Y Points accumulate to unlock levels → Levels unlock benefits → Benefits are issued as vouchers, blind boxes, etc. → Users apply benefits to continued trading**
{% endhint %}

The program is governed by three core principles:

1. **Y Points are permanent.** Y Points are a cumulative growth asset and are not reduced by trading losses, voucher redemption, or benefit usage.
2. **Levels never decrease.** Once a membership level is reached, it is retained long-term and will not be downgraded due to a period of inactivity.
3. **Levels are directly tied to benefits.** Higher levels correspond to larger voucher denominations, higher issuance frequency, and improved odds in blind box draws.

***

### 2. How to Earn Y Points

Y Points can be earned through five categories of tasks covering the full range of user activity on the platform:

| Task Category              | Description                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Onboarding Tasks           | Covers first-time actions such as wallet connection, first deposit, first trade, first insured trade, profile completion, and adding a watchlist item |
| Trading & Fund Usage Tasks | Covers ongoing activity such as insurance purchases, daily trading, multiple trades in a day, consecutive trading days, and large deposit retention   |
| Activity & Referral Tasks  | Covers daily check-in, consecutive check-ins, and referral rewards for a friend's first deposit, first trade, and sustained activity                  |
| Trading Feedback Tasks     | Provides positive incentives for consecutive profitable trades, and corresponding benefit compensation for single or consecutive losses               |
| Event & Seasonal Tasks     | Covers designated trading activity during anniversaries, holidays, birthdays, and limited-time campaigns                                              |

Selected task examples:

| Task                              | Completion Condition                                         | Points Earned           |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| First Wallet Connection           | Successfully connect or create a wallet                      | 50 Y Points             |
| First Deposit                     | Single deposit ≥ 100 USDC                                    | 500 Y Points            |
| First Trade                       | Complete a trade of any type with a notional value ≥ 10 USDC | 500 Y Points            |
| First Insured Trade               | Complete and settle one insured contract trade               | 1,000 Y Points          |
| Daily Check-in                    | Complete check-in on a given day                             | 1 Y Point/day           |
| Referral – Friend's First Deposit | Referred user completes their first deposit                  | 1,000 Y Points/referral |

***

### 3. Membership Levels

YesFi's membership system uses a non-linear V1–V40 level structure, organized into five tiers:

| Tier       | Level Range | User Stage                |
| ---------- | ----------- | ------------------------- |
| Bronze     | V1–V5       | Onboarding Stage          |
| Platinum   | V6–V15      | Active Growth Stage       |
| Black Gold | V16–V25     | Core User Stage           |
| Diamond    | V26–V35     | Long-Term Retention Stage |
| Oracle     | V36–V40     | Elite Status Stage        |

Level progression follows three distinct paces:

* **Early stage (V1–V10):** Low progression thresholds allow users to level up in quick succession by completing onboarding tasks.
* **Mid stage (V11–V30):** Progression depends on sustained trading, check-ins, and event participation, with benefits scaling up correspondingly.
* **Late stage (V31–V40):** Progression thresholds increase significantly, reserved for long-term, highly engaged users, reflecting the exclusivity of these levels.

***

### 4. Membership Benefits

Membership benefits are issued in three forms:

* **Options Vouchers** — used to offset fees related to options trading
* **Contract Insurance Vouchers** — used to offset contract insurance premiums
* **Blind Boxes** — contain a combination of the above two voucher types, with the specific value determined probabilistically

Membership level does not alter the platform's base fee structure. Instead, it affects: benefit issuance frequency, individual voucher denominations, the mix of voucher types, and the probability of receiving higher-value rewards from blind boxes.

Benefit characteristics by stage:

| Stage      | Level Range | Benefit Characteristics                                                                                                                     |
| ---------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Foundation | V1–V5       | Primarily small-denomination options vouchers with limited insurance vouchers; lower blind box frequency                                    |
| Growth     | V6–V15      | Voucher denominations increase progressively; options and insurance vouchers are balanced; blind box frequency increases                    |
| High-Value | V16–V40     | Primarily mid-to-large denomination vouchers; blind box frequency increases significantly; higher probability of large-denomination rewards |

Benefits are issued on a recurring basis, with total benefit value per cycle increasing at higher levels.

***

### 5. Additional Program Features

* **Trading Feedback Mechanism:** Users who achieve consecutive profitable trades may receive additional points or benefit bonuses. In the event of a significant single loss or consecutive losses, the platform issues insurance vouchers as compensation to help reduce the cost of subsequent trades.
* **Reactivation Incentive:** Users who complete a real trade after a period of inactivity will receive a dedicated welcome-back benefit package.
* **Referral Incentive:** Referral rewards extend beyond a friend's first deposit and first trade — if the referred user maintains long-term activity, the referrer receives additional long-term companion benefits.
* **Benefit Freeze and Unfreeze Rules:** If a user has no core activity for 14 consecutive days, their day-to-day benefits will be frozen. Benefits are automatically unfrozen once the user completes any real trade. Freezing does not affect a user's accumulated Y Points or achieved membership level.

***

### 6. Summary

The YesFi membership program uses Y Points as its sole growth metric. Activities such as connecting a wallet, trading, checking in, and referring friends all contribute to point accumulation. Points determine level, and level determines the scope of benefits, which are issued as vouchers and blind boxes to support ongoing trading and platform engagement. Y Points are permanent and membership levels never decrease — ensuring that every user's progress is preserved over time.
