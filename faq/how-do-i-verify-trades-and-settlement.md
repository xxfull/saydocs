# How do I verify trades and settlement?

Verify activity through on-chain records:

* **Contracts and fund flows**: YesFi is an on-chain DEX. Trades and settlement occur on-chain. You control your assets.
* **Trading records**: Review entries, exits, take profit, stop loss, and funding-fee details under **Trading History**, **Positions**, and **Orders**.
* **YesFi Explorer**: Use the official Explorer to check addresses, positions, and trading events. Compare on-chain status with fund flows.
* **Settlement timing**: Perpetual Contract funding settles hourly. Compare your position direction with the current funding rate to determine whether you pay funding that hour.
* **Price source**: Perpetual Contract profit, loss, and liquidation use the mark price. Settlement and risk controls also use the protocol's real-time pricing system.
