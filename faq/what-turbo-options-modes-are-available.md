# What Turbo Options modes are available?

YesFi currently offers seven Turbo Options modes:

* **Trend**: Predict whether an asset price will rise or fall at expiry.
* **Instant**: Predict the price direction over a short period.
* **Range**: Predict whether the expiry price will fall within a specified range.
* **Volatility**: Predict whether price movement will meet a specified condition. Direction does not matter.
* **Pair**: Predict the relative performance of two assets over a specified period.
* **Streak**: Combine predictions across consecutive conditions.
* **Ladder**: Receive different settlement outcomes at different price levels.

Use **Trend** or **Instant** for a clear directional view. Use **Range** or **Volatility** when direction is uncertain. Use **Pair**, **Streak**, or **Ladder** for relative-performance or more complex views.

Product use cases, trigger conditions, and settlement methods vary. Refer to the relevant product page for the applicable rules.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Durations, trigger conditions, payout multipliers, and maximum losses vary by product. Review each item before confirming a trade. Turbo Options are high-risk derivatives and do not guarantee profit.
{% endhint %}
