# How do I deposit and withdraw?

YesFi supports USDC deposits and withdrawals on Ethereum, BSC, Base, and Arbitrum.

### Deposits

Select the correct network and the corresponding USDC contract address. Assets may not arrive if either is incorrect.

### Withdrawals

| Asset | Network  | Minimum withdrawal | Fixed withdrawal fee |
| ----- | -------- | ------------------ | -------------------- |
| USDC  | Arbitrum | 0.2 USDC           | 0.2 USDC             |
| USDC  | Base     | 0.2 USDC           | 0.2 USDC             |
| USDC  | BSC      | 0.2 USDC           | 0.2 USDC             |
| USDC  | Ethereum | 1 USDC             | 1 USDC               |

Withdrawal amounts support up to four decimal places.

Withdrawal fees vary by network. Ethereum has a higher fixed fee. Check the withdrawal page for current fees.
