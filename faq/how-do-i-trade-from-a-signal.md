# How do I trade from a signal?

1. **Open the signal details**: Review the asset, signal type, direction, suggested entry, take profit, stop loss, and generation time.
2. **Check whether the signal remains valid**: Compare the current price with the suggested entry range. Do not chase an order if the price has materially moved.
3. **Select “Place Order”**: YesFi AI pre-fills the asset, direction, entry price, take profit, and stop loss.
4. **Set trading parameters**: Confirm or adjust the amount, leverage, entry price, take profit, and stop loss. Signal parameters are suggestions only.
5. **Review order risk**: Confirm the margin, estimated fees, stop-loss amount, and liquidation risk. Add funds in the **Asset Center** if needed.
6. **Submit the order**: Connect your wallet, review every parameter, then authorize and submit.
7. **Manage the position**: Use the Positions page to review profit and loss, take profit, stop loss, and order status. Hold, adjust, or close as market conditions change.

Use a limit order if the current price has not reached the suggested entry. Return to the default list for active signals if a signal expires or price has materially moved.

A complete trading plan includes entry, exit, timeframe, and position management. Following a signal does not guarantee profit. You make the final decisions on parameters, authorization, and risk.
