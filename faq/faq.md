# FAQ archive

### Question 1: Who is YesFi for?

YesFi is for retail users who want less research and simpler trading.

Users can trade and manage risk on one platform. Before trading, understand the product rules and ensure you can afford to lose your entire investment.

YesFi may be suitable for:

* **Perpetual contract traders**: USDC-margined perpetual contracts support long and short positions, isolated margin, leverage, take profit, and stop loss. They suit experienced traders and users learning perpetual trading.
* **Risk-conscious traders**: Eligible perpetual positions can purchase principal-protection insurance or profit-enhancement insurance. These products help manage liquidation losses or increase target returns. Coverage lasts 24, 72, or 168 hours. Review the purchase page for eligibility requirements.
* **Short-term traders**: Turbo Options offer Trend, Range, Ladder, Streak, Instant, Volatility, and Pair modes. They suit users with a clear view of short-term market movements.
* **Users who need position separation**: Sub-account wallets isolate funds and positions. Users can separate test trades, core positions, and trading strategies.

{% hint style="warning" %}
YesFi is not suitable for users who do not understand derivative risks or cannot afford to lose their entire investment.
{% endhint %}

### Question 2: How do I get started with YesFi?

Start with these three steps:

2. **Choose an account**: Start with your main account. Alternatively, create a sub-account to separate funds and positions.
3. **Choose a trading product**:
   * **Perpetual Contracts** support long and short positions with leverage.

#### Try Turbo Options

1. Open the Options page and select a mode.
2. Use demo trading to understand trading and settlement rules.
3. Check the displayed odds and limits. They update in real time.
4. After understanding the product, consider a small live trade.

#### Make your first Perpetual Contracts trade

1. Select a market, such as BTC or ETH.
2. Choose whether to go long or short.

4. Set take profit and stop loss, if needed.
5. Review the entry price, margin, leverage, and estimated liquidation price.
6. Confirm the details and sign the order.

#### Make your first Turbo Options trade

1. Open the Options trading page.
2. Select a product, such as Trend, Range, or Ladder.
3. Set the market, duration, price level, and premium.
4. Review the live payout multiplier, trading limit, and settlement rules.
5. Confirm the details and submit the order.

New users can use demo trading first. You can also start with a small Trend Options trade. For longer directional trades, consider Perpetual Contracts.

Start with one market, such as BTC. This helps you understand price movements and settlement mechanics.

### Question 8: What Turbo Options modes are available?

YesFi currently offers seven Turbo Options modes:

* **Trend**: Predict whether an asset price will rise or fall at expiry.
* **Instant**: Predict the price direction over a short period.
* **Range**: Predict whether the expiry price will fall within a specified range.
* **Volatility**: Predict whether price movement will meet a specified condition. Direction does not matter.
* **Pair**: Predict the relative performance of two assets over a specified period.
* **Streak**: Combine predictions across consecutive conditions.
* **Ladder**: Receive different settlement outcomes at different price levels.

Use **Trend** or **Instant** for a clear directional view. Use **Range** or **Volatility** when direction is uncertain. Use **Pair**, **Streak**, or **Ladder** for relative-performance or more complex views.

Product use cases, trigger conditions, and settlement methods vary. Refer to the relevant product page for the applicable rules.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Durations, trigger conditions, payout multipliers, and maximum losses vary by product. Review each item before confirming a trade. Turbo Options are high-risk derivatives and do not guarantee profit.
{% endhint %}

### Question 9: How do modes differ in duration, triggers, and risk?

Modes mainly differ in three ways: observation period, prediction condition, and number of variables. YesFi offers Instant durations of 5 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, and 5 minutes. Check the order page for the available durations and parameters.

| Product    | Duration                                      | Trigger condition                                                                | Main risk                                                                             |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trend      | Usually observes a complete period            | At expiry, the price rises or falls relative to the start price and price spread | Near the start price, a small reverse move may invalidate the condition               |
| Instant    | The shortest period, often seconds or minutes | At the end of the period, price direction meets the selected condition           | Highly sensitive to instant moves and entry timing                                    |
| Range      | Observes the expiry period set in the order   | At expiry, price is inside or outside a specified range                          | A broadly correct view may still fail if price crosses a boundary                     |
| Volatility | Covers short or longer observation periods    | Price movement meets or does not meet a defined threshold                        | Direction does not matter; too little or too much movement can fail                   |
| Pair       | Both assets use the same period               | Compares the relative performance of two assets                                  | Relative strength matters, even if both assets rise or fall                           |
| Streak     | Covers multiple consecutive stages            | Multiple conditions are met in sequence or together                              | One unmet condition may affect settlement; more conditions are usually harder to meet |
| Ladder     | One period with multiple price levels         | Settlement follows the price level reached at expiry                             | Higher levels are usually harder to reach; results differ near a level boundary       |

#### Trigger methods

* **Expiry-based**: Only the final price at settlement matters.
* **Path-based**: Touching a condition during the observation period may trigger it.

Rules on the confirmation page take precedence. Do not infer rules from a mode name alone. Shorter durations are more sensitive to short-term price changes.

Before confirming, check the entry price, duration, trigger method, settlement price source, payout multiplier, and maximum loss.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If a condition is not met, you may lose the entire amount paid for that trade.
{% endhint %}

### Question 10: How do Turbo Options differ from standard options?

Turbo Options and standard options are both derivatives. Their structures and trading flows differ.

Turbo Options use predefined market conditions. You choose an asset, duration, and prediction, then wait for automatic settlement. Standard options require a call or put, strike price, expiry date, and contract quantity. You also manage the premium and position.

| Comparison           | Turbo Options                                                          | Standard options                                                                              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Settlement speed** | Shorter windows and a faster pace, often seconds or brief periods      | Longer periods provide more time to observe price movements and volatility                    |
| **Trading approach** | Captures short-term moves, instant direction, and rapid range changes  | Supports fuller short-term views, including direction, range, movement, and relative strength |
| **Experience**       | Designed for quick entries and exits                                   | Choose an asset and duration after understanding the rules                                    |
| **Risk profile**     | Shorter duration gives less room for price movement and increases pace | More time for assessment, but short-term volatility risk remains                              |
| **Shared risk**      | An incorrect prediction may lose the entire premium                    | An incorrect prediction may lose the entire premium                                           |

Choose Turbo Options for faster, shorter windows. Choose standard options for more time and more complete trading structures.

Standard options give the holder the right to buy or sell an asset at an agreed price. Exercise and settlement depend on the contract rules. Turbo Options are closer to selecting a predefined condition and waiting for settlement.

Simpler trading does not reduce risk. Before trading, confirm the duration, trigger method, settlement price, payout multiplier, and maximum possible loss. The order confirmation page defines the applicable YesFi Turbo Options rules.

### Question 11: What types of Perpetual Contract signals are available?

YesFi AI generates three main types of Perpetual Contract signals:

* **News signals**: YesFi AI analyzes macroeconomic data, policy changes, project announcements, and market events. It identifies potential trading opportunities that may affect contract prices.
* **Technical analysis signals**: YesFi AI uses price trends, volume, moving averages, RSI, MACD, support, and resistance. It generates bullish or bearish signals.
* **KOL opinion signals**: YesFi AI collects and analyzes trading views from market KOLs. It structures the referenced assets, direction, and key prices into signals.

Each signal shows its type, asset, direction, suggested entry price, take profit, stop loss, generation time, and validity status. Use these details to assess its source and rationale.

Each signal type has limits. News may already be priced in. Technical indicators may lag. KOL views may contain subjective bias.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Signals support decisions and do not guarantee profit. Use multiple information sources. Independently assess your position size, leverage, and maximum affordable loss.
{% endhint %}

### Question 12: How do I trade from a signal?

1. **Open the signal details**: Review the asset, signal type, direction, suggested entry, take profit, stop loss, and generation time.
2. **Check whether the signal remains valid**: Compare the current price with the suggested entry range. Do not chase an order if the price has materially moved.
3. **Select “Place Order”**: YesFi AI pre-fills the asset, direction, entry price, take profit, and stop loss.
4. **Set trading parameters**: Confirm or adjust the amount, leverage, entry price, take profit, and stop loss. Signal parameters are suggestions only.
5. **Review order risk**: Confirm the margin, estimated fees, stop-loss amount, and liquidation risk. Add funds in the **Asset Center** if needed.
6. **Submit the order**: Connect your wallet, review every parameter, then authorize and submit.
7. **Manage the position**: Use the Positions page to review profit and loss, take profit, stop loss, and order status. Hold, adjust, or close as market conditions change.

Use a limit order if the current price has not reached the suggested entry. Return to the default list for active signals if a signal expires or price has materially moved.

A complete trading plan includes entry, exit, timeframe, and position management. Following a signal does not guarantee profit. You make the final decisions on parameters, authorization, and risk.

Yes, if the relevant Perpetual Contract position is eligible.

To add insurance to a signal trade:

Trading insurance only applies to eligible Perpetual Contract positions. It does not apply to options signals.

### Question 14: What is Principal Protection Insurance?

Principal Protection Insurance is a trading protection feature attached to a Perpetual Contract position. It is not traditional insurance.

If an insured position is liquidated, it pays the full opening margin for that position. This helps prevent the margin from falling to zero after liquidation.

It may help users who:

* Use leverage and want protection against losing their full margin.
* Trade during extreme market movements.
* Hold short-term positions in highly volatile markets.

Principal Protection Insurance supports selected crypto assets, stocks, indices, and commodities. Coverage is available for 24, 72, or 168 hours. Check the insurance confirmation page for premiums, trigger conditions, supported assets, and payout rules.

### Question 15: What is Profit Enhancement Insurance?

Profit Enhancement Insurance is an optional trading protection feature attached to a Perpetual Contract position.

When floating profit meets the required condition, the insurance adds an equal amount of floating profit. In effect, it can increase profit from 100% to 200%.

It may suit users who expect a correct trade to retrace. It aims to preserve the upside after gains occur.

You can cancel the insurance at any time. The remaining premium is refunded based on the remaining coverage time. Check the insurance page for the applicable terms.

Profit Enhancement Insurance supports selected crypto assets, stocks, indices, and commodities. Coverage is available for 24, 72, or 168 hours. Check the confirmation page for premiums, payout rules, supported assets, and eligibility.

### Question 16: How do I buy and manage trading insurance?

Trading insurance is a Perpetual Contract position feature. It is not traditional insurance.

* **Principal Protection Insurance** pays the full opening margin after liquidation.
* **Profit Enhancement Insurance** adds an equal amount of floating profit when conditions are met.
* **Coverage** lasts 24, 72, or 168 hours (7 days).
* **Assets** include selected crypto assets, stocks, indices, and commodities.

To purchase insurance:

1. Select a Perpetual Contract position.
2. Select an insurance type and coverage period.
3. Review the premium, trigger conditions, and expected payout.
4. Confirm and sign.

You can manage insurance in these ways:

* **Cancel**: End coverage at any time. The remaining premium is refunded according to the remaining period.
* **Change coverage**: Switch between 24, 72, and 168 hours. The unused original premium is refunded according to the remaining period.
* **Close a position**: The insurance ends with the position. The unused premium is refunded according to the remaining period.

Check the product page for real-time premiums, eligibility, supported assets, insurance types, and payout conditions. YesFi AI cannot place trades or purchase insurance. It can help assess a suitable insurance type.

### Question 17: Can I change or cancel trading insurance after it starts?

Yes. You can cancel insurance at any time. The platform calculates the refund for unused coverage based on the remaining time and applicable rules.

You can also switch between 24-hour, 72-hour, and 168-hour coverage. The unused original premium is refunded based on the remaining coverage time.

When you close the linked Perpetual Contract position, its insurance ends automatically. The unused premium is refunded based on the remaining coverage time.

Trading insurance only applies to Perpetual Contract positions. It does not apply to Turbo Options.

### Question 18: Does Turbo Options support trading insurance?

Turbo Options do not currently support trading insurance.

Principal Protection Insurance and Profit Enhancement Insurance only apply to Perpetual Contract positions. Turbo Options are short-duration options products. Their trading mechanics are separate from Perpetual Contract insurance.

Perpetual Contract insurance offers 24-hour, 72-hour, and 168-hour coverage. These periods do not apply to Turbo Options.

Before trading Turbo Options, choose a premium amount that matches your risk tolerance.

### Question 19: How do I verify trades and settlement?

Verify activity through on-chain records:

* **Contracts and fund flows**: YesFi is an on-chain DEX. Trades and settlement occur on-chain. You control your assets.
* **Trading records**: Review entries, exits, take profit, stop loss, and funding-fee details under **Trading History**, **Positions**, and **Orders**.
* **YesFi Explorer**: Use the official Explorer to check addresses, positions, and trading events. Compare on-chain status with fund flows.
* **Settlement timing**: Perpetual Contract funding settles hourly. Compare your position direction with the current funding rate to determine whether you pay funding that hour.
* **Price source**: Perpetual Contract profit, loss, and liquidation use the mark price. Settlement and risk controls also use the protocol's real-time pricing system.
