# What is Profit Enhancement Insurance?

Profit Enhancement Insurance is an optional trading protection feature attached to a Perpetual Contract position.

When floating profit meets the required condition, the insurance adds an equal amount of floating profit. In effect, it can increase profit from 100% to 200%.

It may suit users who expect a correct trade to retrace. It aims to preserve the upside after gains occur.

You can cancel the insurance at any time. The remaining premium is refunded based on the remaining coverage time. Check the insurance page for the applicable terms.

Profit Enhancement Insurance supports selected crypto assets, stocks, indices, and commodities. Coverage is available for 24, 72, or 168 hours. Check the confirmation page for premiums, payout rules, supported assets, and eligibility.
