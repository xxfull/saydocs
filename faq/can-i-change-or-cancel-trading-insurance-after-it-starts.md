# Can I change or cancel trading insurance after it starts?

Yes. You can cancel insurance at any time. The platform calculates the refund for unused coverage based on the remaining time and applicable rules.

You can also switch between 24-hour, 72-hour, and 168-hour coverage. The unused original premium is refunded based on the remaining coverage time.

When you close the linked Perpetual Contract position, its insurance ends automatically. The unused premium is refunded based on the remaining coverage time.

Trading insurance only applies to Perpetual Contract positions. It does not apply to Turbo Options.
