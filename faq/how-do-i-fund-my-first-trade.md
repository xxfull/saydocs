# How do I fund my first trade?

Before depositing, check the supported assets and networks in the **Asset Center**. Ensure your connected wallet or AA wallet has enough available assets.

Use the deposit address shown on the page. Send assets to the relevant trading account or contract settlement environment. You can also deposit directly through a connected wallet.

For your first deposit, send a small test amount. Confirm the asset type, network, and address before sending more funds.

After your deposit arrives, you can trade Perpetual Contracts, Turbo Options, and other available products.
