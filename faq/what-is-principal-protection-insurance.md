# What is Principal Protection Insurance?

Principal Protection Insurance is a trading protection feature attached to a Perpetual Contract position. It is not traditional insurance.

If an insured position is liquidated, it pays the full opening margin for that position. This helps prevent the margin from falling to zero after liquidation.

It may help users who:

* Use leverage and want protection against losing their full margin.
* Trade during extreme market movements.
* Hold short-term positions in highly volatile markets.

Principal Protection Insurance supports selected crypto assets, stocks, indices, and commodities. Coverage is available for 24, 72, or 168 hours. Check the insurance confirmation page for premiums, trigger conditions, supported assets, and payout rules.
