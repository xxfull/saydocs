# Beats

### **Rules**

* Choose any asset and predict whether its price will go up or down after a set period. If correct, you win a large payout; if wrong, you lose your entire premium.
* Minimum amount: 1 USDC; Maximum amount: 5,000 USDC
* Available assets: BTC, ETH, SOL, Gold, Crude Oil, USD, JPY, EUR (24 hours); NVIDIA, SanDisk, SPCX (US market hours), etc.

### **How It Works**

* The user selects a premium amount, an asset, and a target price range within an expected timeframe to create an order.
* Settlement occurs when the timeframe expires. If the price lands within the user's predicted range, the payout is delivered — for example: 3.4 × 50 = 170 USDC. If the price does not reach the predicted range, the user loses the entire premium for that order.

### **Payout Structure**

* Payouts increase as the timeframe gets longer.&#x20;
* Payouts also increase the further the target price range is from the current price.
