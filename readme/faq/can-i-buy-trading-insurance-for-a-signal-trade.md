# Can I buy trading insurance for a signal trade?

Yes, if the relevant Perpetual Contract position is eligible.

YesFi trading insurance — Principal Protection Insurance and Profit Enhancement Insurance — attaches to Perpetual Contract positions. It supports selected crypto assets, stocks, indices, and commodities. Check the product page for supported assets and insurance types.

* Trading insurance is attached to a Perpetual Contract position. It is not a standalone options product.
* Coverage periods are 24 hours, 72 hours, or 168 hours (7 days).
* Eligibility, premiums, and payout conditions appear on the insurance confirmation page.

To add insurance to a signal trade:

1. Check whether the signal asset supports trading insurance.
2. Open a position on the Perpetual Contracts page.
3. Check whether insurance is available for that position.

Trading insurance only applies to eligible Perpetual Contract positions. It does not apply to options signals.
