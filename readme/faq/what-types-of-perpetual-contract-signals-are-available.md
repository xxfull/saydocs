# What types of Perpetual Contract signals are available?

YesFi AI generates three main types of Perpetual Contract signals:

* **News signals**: YesFi AI analyzes macroeconomic data, policy changes, project announcements, and market events. It identifies potential trading opportunities that may affect contract prices.
* **Technical analysis signals**: YesFi AI uses price trends, volume, moving averages, RSI, MACD, support, and resistance. It generates bullish or bearish signals.
* **KOL opinion signals**: YesFi AI collects and analyzes trading views from market KOLs. It structures the referenced assets, direction, and key prices into signals.

Each signal shows its type, asset, direction, suggested entry price, take profit, stop loss, generation time, and validity status. Use these details to assess its source and rationale.

Each signal type has limits. News may already be priced in. Technical indicators may lag. KOL views may contain subjective bias.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Signals support decisions and do not guarantee profit. Use multiple information sources. Independently assess your position size, leverage, and maximum affordable loss.
{% endhint %}
