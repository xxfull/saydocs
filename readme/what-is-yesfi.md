# What is YesFi

YesFi 是新一代 AI 原生永續合約交易所，以自然語言對話為核心交互，重新定義了鏈上衍生性商品的交易體驗。使用者無需面對密集的圖表介面與複雜的參數設置，無需自行研判入場時機，只需透過對話向 AI 交易 Agent 表達意圖，或一鍵跟隨精選策略訊號即可直接下單。

YesFi is a next-generation AI-native perpetual contract exchange that redefines the on-chain derivatives trading experience with natural language dialogue as its core interaction method. Users no longer need to deal with dense chart interfaces and complex parameter settings, nor do they need to judge entry timing themselves. They can simply express their intentions to the AI ​​trading agent through dialogue or place orders directly by following selected strategy signals with one click.

***

### Core concept

> **One Yes, Trade All**
>
> 無需考慮標的、策略，無需理解任何底層參數，任何經驗水平的用戶只需對 AI Agent 的執行方案說一聲 Yes，從開倉、調倉到止損止盈的完整倉位生命週期，全部由 AI 負責。
>
> No need to consider index or strategies, no need to understand any underlying parameters; users of any experience level simply need to say "Yes" to the AI ​​Agent's execution plan, and the entire position lifecycle, from opening a position and adjusting it to stop-loss and take-profit, will be handled entirely by the AI.

***

### What you get in YesFi

#### AI trading agent 💎

AI 交易 Agent 透過對話引導你完成每一個交易環節——從釐清交易意圖，到下單、調倉、查看風險敞口。Agent 結合市場數據、交易信號與你的歷史交易行為，提供個性化的入場建議、加倉策略與期權比較。你始終保有完整的掌控權：任何操作均需你明確指示或確認後方可執行，Agent 不會自動代替你進行任何交易。你只需用自然語言描述方向、大概的保證金規模與止盈止損意圖——支援文字輸入，部分客戶端亦支援語音——Agent 負責將其轉化為具體可執行的訂單動作。

The AI trading agent guides you through every step via chat — from clarifying your intent to placing orders, adjusting positions, and reviewing risk exposure. Drawing on market data, trading signals, and your historical behavior, the agent offers entry suggestions, position-sizing strategies, and option comparisons tailored to your context. You stay in full control at all times: no action is taken without your explicit instruction or confirmation, and the agent never trades automatically on your behalf. Simply describe your direction, rough margin size, and TP/SL intent in plain language — text or voice — and the agent handles the translation into concrete, executable actions.

#### Strategies and Trading Signals 💎

極速選擇權與合約交易訊號共同構成 YesFi 的核心入場工具。前者精選 BTC、ETH 等主流資產策略，以卡片形式呈現觸發價格、賠率與結算週期，支持做多、做空、雙向突破，策略由 AI 動態篩選，一鍵下單即可鎖定風險收益比；後者融合市場情緒與鏈上數據，即時推送附有策略邏輯說明的交易訊號，訊號涵蓋方向、槓桿、止盈等完整參數，一鍵直達執行，從訊號到部位無縫銜接。

YesFi's core entry tools consist of fast options and contract trading signals. The former selects strategies for mainstream assets such as BTC and ETH, presenting trigger prices, odds, and settlement cycles in card format. It supports going long, going short, and two-way breakouts. The strategies are dynamically selected by AI, and one-click order placement locks in the risk-reward ratio. The latter integrates market sentiment and on-chain data, pushing trading signals in real time with explanations of the strategy logic. The signals cover complete parameters such as direction, leverage, and profit-taking, and can be executed with one click, seamlessly connecting signals to positions.

#### Insurance features 💎

Protect capital. Expand upside.

* **Zero Loss Insurance**: for covered positions, **if liquidation happens, the product pays the full initial margin for that position according to the policy terms**. In effect, the full margin is restored. **It is currently available for 20 major perpetual pairs including BTC and ETH, plus commodities and FX**. Coverage terms are **24h / 72h / 168h (7 days)**.
* **Double Profit Insurance**: once floating PnL **reaches the target**, the product adds **an equal amount of extra floating profit**. This is often described as **100% → 200%**. Premiums are **priced in real time** based on market conditions, and **cancellation with refund follows product rules**. It is also available for **20 major perpetual pairs including BTC and ETH, plus commodities and FX**, with terms of **24h / 72h / 168h (7 days)**.

Premiums, trigger conditions, and refund rules **always follow the confirmation screen and official documentation**. Do not rely on unofficial claims from external channels.

#### USDC perpetuals across multiple asset classes

Beyond major crypto assets, YesFi also supports **USDC-settled perpetuals** across precious metals, energy, FX, indices, and commodities. Supported markets depend on the live platform listing. You can ask the AI agent which symbols are currently available.
