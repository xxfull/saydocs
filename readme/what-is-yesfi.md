# What is YesFi

YesFi is the first all-asset derivatives platform bringing Flash Options and perps to crypto, FX, stocks, commodities, and more.

YesFi makes trading simple through three product lines: **Options** for fixed-payout trades, **Predict** for short-term market predictions, and **Perps** for leveraged trading, AI Signals, and Insurance.

\
YesFi turns complex trading decisions and execution into one simple confirmation: Yes. That makes "**One Yes, Trade All**." not just a slogan, but the everyday trading experience on YesFi.

***

### What you get in YesFi

#### **Options 💎**

YesFi offers three lines of options. All three are buy-only: you pay up front, the most you can lose is what you paid, and there is no margin or liquidation. All settle in USDC.

* [**Flash Options**](https://docs.yesfi.com/flash-options): fixed-payout predictions that settle in seconds to minutes, across seven modes.
* [**Regular Options**](../options/regular-options.md): listed BTC calls and puts, with expiries from one day to several months.
* [**0DTE Options**](../options/0dte-options.md): Regular Options on their last day, for a low-cost position on today’s move.

#### Predict **💎**

YesFi offers two lines of prediction products. Both are buy-only: you pay upfront, the most you can lose is what you paid, and there is no margin or liquidation. All settle in USDC.

* [**Candle Streak**](../predict/candle-streak.md): predict the direction of six consecutive 10-second BTC candles within a 60-second round. Choose Count Only to predict how many candles move up, down or stay flat, or Exact Order to predict the direction of each candle in sequence.
* [**Peak Performance**](../predict/peak-performance.md): predict which of the six candles will mark the round's highest or lowest price. Choose Single to predict either the high or low candle, or Pair to predict both.

#### **Perps Signals 💎**

YesFi organizes real-time perps signals into three categories:

* **Market news:** AI analyzes market-moving news, sentiment, and on-chain activity to identify timely trading opportunities.
* **Technical analysis:** Signals use price action, indicators, and market structure to define potential direction, entries, and risk levels.
* **KOL views:** Follow actionable market views from selected traders and analysts, with their historical performance records.

Every signal includes direction, leverage, take-profit, and stop-loss parameters. Review the strategy rationale before making your own trading decision.

#### **Insurance features** 💎

* **Zero Loss Insurance**: for covered positions, if liquidation happens, the product pays the full initial margin for that position according to the policy terms. In effect, the full margin is restored. It is currently available for 20 major perpetual pairs including BTC and ETH, plus commodities and FX. Coverage terms are **24h / 72h / 168h (7 days).**
* **Double Profit Insurance**: once floating PnL reaches the target, the product adds an equal amount of extra floating profit. This is often described as **100% → 200%**. Premiums are priced in real time based on market conditions, and cancellation with refund follows product rules. It is also available for 20 major perpetual pairs including BTC and ETH, plus commodities and FX, with terms of **24h / 72h / 168h (7 days)**.

> Premiums, trigger conditions, and refund rules are governed solely by the confirmation page at the time of purchase and official documentation. Do not rely on any unofficial claims from third-party sources.

#### **AI assistant** 💎

The AI assistant helps you explore markets through chat. It uses market data, trading signals, and your historical behavior to provide trade ideas, risk context, position-sizing guidance, and Flash Options comparisons. Describe your market view, rough margin size, and TP/SL intent in plain language — text or voice — to receive relevant analysis and explanations.

#### USDC perpetuals across multiple asset classes

Beyond major crypto assets, YesFi also supports **USDC-settled perpetuals** across precious metals, energy, FX, indices, and commodities. Supported markets depend on the live platform listing. Ask the AI assistant which symbols are currently available.
